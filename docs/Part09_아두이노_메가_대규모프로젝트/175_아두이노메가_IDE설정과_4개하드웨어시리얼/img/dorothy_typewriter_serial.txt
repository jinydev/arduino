Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. Conceptual typewriter metaphor for Arduino Serial Monitor text transmission and line feeds. Characters: one young girl with braided pigtails in denim overalls (Dorothy), one cute black-and-white tuxedo cat with glasses (Assistant Jiny), and one fluffy puppy with a sky blue bandana (Toto). On a wooden laboratory desk, an enchanting retro mechanical typewriter is wired with glowing fiber cables to an Arduino Uno board. Dorothy joyfully presses typewriter keys typing Hello Arduino! As she taps, paper scrolls upward with neat typed text and a tiny bell chimes. Assistant Jiny wears round glasses and inspects the printed message ribbon with satisfaction. Toto rests his paws on the desk edge wagging his tail happily. Clean crisp outline art, cell shading, gentle studio lighting, pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece.
