Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. Transmitting international Morse code SOS distress signals (... --- ...) using an active buzzer and Arduino Uno. Characters: one young girl with braided pigtails in denim overalls (Dorothy), one cute black-and-white tuxedo cat with glasses (Assistant Jiny), and one fluffy puppy with a sky blue bandana (Toto). On a wooden laboratory desk, an active buzzer connected to digital pin 4 of an Arduino Uno beeps rhythmic audio sparks into the air with dots and dashes. Dorothy taps out the rhythm with a cheerful adventurous smile. Assistant Jiny holds an ancient parchment chart explaining Morse Code: S equals three short beeps, O equals three long beeps. Toto wears a tiny sailor captain hat and salutes proudly.
