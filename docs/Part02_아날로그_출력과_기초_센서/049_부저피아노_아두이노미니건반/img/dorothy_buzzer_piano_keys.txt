Ghibli 2D anime style, a joyful musical maker project showing Dorothy playing an Arduino mini piano made of 7 push buttons on a breadboard. In the center, a long white breadboard hosts a neat row of 7 colorful tactile buttons arranged like piano keys (labeled C, D, E, F, G, A, B). Dorothy, an adorable 11-year-old girl with braided brown pigtails in a sky-blue dress, is happily playing the button keys with both hands. A passive buzzer connected to the Arduino Uno emits a cascade of bright, luminous musical notes floating joyfully upward. Cute puppy Toto wags his tail rhythmically to the melody, and assistant cat Jiny wearing round glasses watches admiringly holding an open sheet-music notebook. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece, 16:9 aspect ratio.
