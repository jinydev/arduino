Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. Conceptual metaphor of UART Serial communication with TX and RX cross connection (Transmit and Receive). Characters: one young girl with braided pigtails in denim overalls (Dorothy), and one cute black-and-white tuxedo cat with glasses (Assistant Jiny). On a clean white stage, two cute communication stations represent two communicating microcontrollers. Two glowing color-coded cables cross each other cleanly: a red cable from Station A mouth (TX) connects to Station B ear (RX), and a blue cable from Station B mouth (TX) connects to Station A ear (RX). Dorothy speaks joyfully into a retro acoustic tin-can microphone labeled TX, while listening to an earpiece labeled RX. Assistant Jiny holds the complementary can on the other side, taking notes with a smile. Clean crisp outline art, cell shading, gentle studio lighting, pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece.
