Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. Conceptual metaphor of PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) magic creating virtual analog brightness on an Arduino Uno. Characters: one adult male mentor with glasses and navy jacket (Professor Hojin), one young girl with braided pigtails in denim overalls (Dorothy), and one cute black-and-white tuxedo cat with glasses and golden bell (Assistant Jiny). On a neat laboratory workbench, an Arduino Uno board emits magical glowing square pulses that smoothly blend into a soft, warm continuous analog light halo. Professor Hojin gestures gently explaining how rapid digital pulses simulate analog voltage. Dorothy looks with wide sparkling eyes in wonder. Assistant Jiny holds a miniature wand pointing at the duty cycle wave. Clean crisp outline art, cell shading, gentle studio lighting, pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece.
