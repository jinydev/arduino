An exciting science lab robotics demonstration of DC motor direction control. On a wooden workbench, an authentic turquoise Arduino Uno is wired to a red L298N motor driver with its black heatsink. On a small breadboard, two colorful arcade-style push buttons (a green 'FORWARD' button and an orange 'REVERSE' button) are connected to Arduino input pins. When Dorothy presses the green button, a yellow geared DC motor with a patterned spinner disc spins clockwise with a green directional arrow; when she presses the orange button, it instantly reverses counter-clockwise with a yellow arrow.
