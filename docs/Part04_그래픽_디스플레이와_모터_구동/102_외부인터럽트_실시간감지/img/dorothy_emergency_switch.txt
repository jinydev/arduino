An exciting science lab robotics demonstration of hardware external interrupt emergency stop. On a wooden workbench, an authentic turquoise Arduino Uno is wired via digital pin 2 (INT0) to a large industrial red mushroom emergency-stop button with a yellow protective collar. When Dorothy slams down her palm onto the red emergency button, a high-speed spinning robot motor stops instantly within 1 microsecond (FALLING edge trigger), while a red warning beacon light flashes. Dorothy (enthusiastic 11-year-old girl student, twin braided pigtails with colorful beads, blue denim dungarees with orange striped shirt, red sneakers) breathes a sigh of joyful relief that the emergency interrupt worked without delay. Assistant Jiny (clever tuxedo cat with round glasses and red collar) points with his soft paw at pin D2 on the Arduino. Toto (scruffy little Cairn terrier puppy, dark brindle fur, curious shiny eyes, perky ears, red fabric collar) sits safely beside the stopped motor.
