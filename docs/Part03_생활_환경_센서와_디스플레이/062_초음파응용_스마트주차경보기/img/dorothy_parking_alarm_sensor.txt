Ghibli 2D anime style, an exciting and realistic maker project showing Dorothy and puppy Toto testing an Arduino smart parking assist system (PAS). On a clean white tabletop, a cute toy car chassis has an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor mounted on its rear bumper, backing up toward a wooden wall. As the car nears the wall, an Arduino Uno triggers rapid warning beeps from a buzzer and flashes a bright red warning LED. Dorothy, an 11-year-old girl with braided pigtails in a sky-blue dress, holds a mini remote controller carefully maneuvering the car. Cute black puppy Toto stands behind the car acting like a traffic director raising a paw saying Stop. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece, 16:9 aspect ratio.
