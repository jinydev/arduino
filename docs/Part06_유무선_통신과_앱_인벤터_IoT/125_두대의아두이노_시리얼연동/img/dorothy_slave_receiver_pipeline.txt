An educational conceptual illustration of the Slave Receiver Arduino processing pipeline. A wise grey cat mentor Genie points with a magical wand at an incoming character packet '1' arriving into a glowing 64-byte circular ring buffer mailbox inside the Arduino. From the mailbox, code reads the character and triggers digital pin 8 to emit a bright golden light beam turning on a bright red LED lamp. Dorothy looks thrilled.
