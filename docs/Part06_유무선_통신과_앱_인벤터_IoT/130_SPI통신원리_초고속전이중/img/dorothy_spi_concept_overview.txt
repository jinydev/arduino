Studio Ghibli 2D anime style, 2D animation aesthetic, clean crisp anime line art, vibrant soft flat cel shading, storybook lighting, masterpiece, highly detailed. An educational conceptual illustration of high-speed Full-Duplex SPI communication. Master Arduino and Slave device exchange data simultaneously along two opposing glowing light beams: blue beam MOSI flowing from Master to Slave, and golden beam MISO flowing from Slave to Master at the exact same moment synchronized by a pulsing clock rhythm. Dorothy and brindle dog Toto watch the high-speed data flow in wonder. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, 16:9 aspect ratio.
