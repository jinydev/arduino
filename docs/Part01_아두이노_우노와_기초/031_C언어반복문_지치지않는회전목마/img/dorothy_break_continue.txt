Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. Conceptual metaphor for loop jump commands: emergency break and skip continue. Exactly four characters: one adult male mentor, one young girl, one cat, one dog. On a carousel track, two distinct railway mechanisms are demonstrated: a red emergency brake lever labeled break; that immediately stops the carousel and exits outside, and a green bypass switch labeled continue; that skips only the current lap and jumps straight to the next lap. Professor Hojin with glasses explains early termination versus skip. Dorothy in denim overalls inspects both control levers with keen interest. Assistant Jiny, the tuxedo cat with glasses and bell, demonstrates pulling the miniature lever. Toto the white puppy with sky blue bandana sits happily near the exit gate.
