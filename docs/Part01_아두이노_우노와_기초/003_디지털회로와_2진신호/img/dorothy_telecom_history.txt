Historical educational panorama about the evolution of communication signals into digital binary code.
In the center, a kind Korean male professor (Prof. Hojin, reference 1) with black styled hair, glasses, navy jacket, presenting a colorful historical timeline scroll.
The illustrated timeline shows ancient mountaintop beacon fires (smoke/fire signal), mechanical optical telegraph tower, Samuel Morse's telegraph key, and modern glowing computer microchips with 0 and 1.
Beside him, an inspired 9-year-old girl (Dorothy, reference 2) with brown braided pigtails in denim overalls listens with wide amazed eyes.
A tuxedo bicolour cat with glasses and bell collar (Jiny the lab assistant cat, reference 3) points to a small vintage telegraph key on the table.
Beside Dorothy, a fluffy white bichon dog with a sky blue bandana (Toto) sits happily looking at the historical timeline.
Masterpiece, epic history of telecommunication, warm nostalgic anime art.
