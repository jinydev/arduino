Educational scene revealing the inside metal spring clips of a solderless breadboard.
On a clean table, an oversized cutaway model of a breadboard shows the internal metallic spring clips: horizontal power bus rails on the sides (red +, blue -) and vertical 5-hole terminal strips in the center separated by a middle ravine.
A kind Korean male professor (Prof. Hojin, reference 1) with black styled hair, glasses, navy jacket, holds up a single metal spring clip with tweezers to demonstrate how it grips component wire leads.
Beside him, an attentive 9-year-old girl (Dorothy, reference 2) in denim overalls peers through a magnifying glass with an enlightened smile.
A smart tuxedo bicolour cat with glasses and bell collar (Jiny the lab assistant cat, reference 3) points its paw along a 5-hole vertical strip.
