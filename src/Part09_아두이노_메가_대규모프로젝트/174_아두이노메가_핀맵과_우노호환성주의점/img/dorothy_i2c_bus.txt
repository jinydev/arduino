Ghibli 2D anime style, an elegant embedded architecture scene explaining the magic of I2C two-wire bus communication. In the center, an Arduino Uno (acting as Master) connects to an open high-speed data bus consisting of just two glowing colorful lines: SDA (Serial Data) and SCL (Serial Clock). Multiple smart peripheral modules (an LCD display, an RTC clock chip, an environmental sensor, and an IMU motion sensor) hang neatly in parallel along these two shared wires, each marked with a tiny glowing hexadecimal address tag (0x27, 0x68). Professor Hojin, an intelligent friendly Korean professor in glasses, points toward the elegant bus topology with a pointer. Dorothy, an enthusiastic 11-year-old girl in a blue dress with braided pigtails, smiles in amazement alongside smart assistant cat Jiny in round glasses. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece, 16:9 aspect ratio.
