Ghibli 2D anime style, an imaginative smart home maker project showing Dorothy and puppy Toto testing an Arduino raindrop sensor and automatic motorized window. On a clean tabletop, a miniature model house window frame is fitted with a small servo motor. Outside the window, raindrops fall gently onto a square nickel-plated raindrop sensor plate with zigzag traces. As soon as droplets land, the Arduino Uno activates the servo to close the window automatically. Dorothy, a cheerful 11-year-old girl with braided pigtails in a sky-blue dress, holds a small yellow umbrella in delight. Cute black puppy Toto sits happily inside, safe from the rain. A miniature diagram shows how water droplets bridge the gap between traces. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece, 16:9 aspect ratio.
