Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. A bright modern tech classroom where Professor Hojin, Assistant Jiny, Dorothy, and Toto are learning about Arduino serial communication and reading serial input from keyboard.
On the wooden workbench, Dorothy is typing enthusiastically on a computer keyboard connected to an Arduino Uno board. Professor Hojin in navy jacket, light gray shirt, and glasses points toward the monitor displaying serial terminal characters. Jiny, the cute black-and-white tuxedo cat wearing small round glasses and a golden bell collar, holds a magnifying glass inspecting glowing digital letter packets flowing along the USB cable into a glowing mailbox icon on the Arduino. Toto the small fluffy white bichon dog with a sky blue bandana sits excitedly watching Dorothy type.
