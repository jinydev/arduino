Ghibli 2D anime style, a whimsical and heartwarming educational scene about Arduino input listening. In the center, a charming Arduino Uno board personified with cute attentive listening ears listening to signals from external sensor wires and a yellow push button. Professor Hojin, a friendly Korean male professor in glasses and a navy cardigan, smiles as he explains the concept of listening (INPUT mode). Dorothy, an adorable 11-year-old girl in a blue dress with braided pigtails, cups her hand around her ear imitating listening, while cute black puppy Toto perches his ears up attentively. Soft glowing input signal pulses (0 and 1) gently flow from the button toward the Arduino pins. Pure solid clean white background, soft floor shadow, no cluttered walls, no speech bubble, no text, no dialogue balloon, high resolution, masterpiece, 16:9 aspect ratio.
