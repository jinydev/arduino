Studio Ghibli style 2D anime illustration. A continuous breathing loop cycle (fade-in, hold, fade-out, rest) of an Arduino LED. Characters: one young girl with braided pigtails in denim overalls (Dorothy), one cute black-and-white tuxedo cat with glasses (Assistant Jiny), and one fluffy puppy with a sky blue bandana (Toto). On a circular wooden table, an elegant circular glowing ring shows a continuous smooth loop: breathing in (brightening), holding breath, and breathing out (dimming). Dorothy inhales and exhales in sync with the light with serene joy. Assistant Jiny guides the cycle rhythm with a metronome. Toto wags his tail happily following the calm light wave.
